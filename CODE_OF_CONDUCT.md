# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Critique the work, not the person.

## What is expected

- Review the code, the question or the wording — never the author.
- Assume a mistake before bad faith. Most wrong answers are someone learning.
- Accept "no" without re-litigating it. A maintainer's call is not an invitation
  to argue it twice.
- Say what you mean plainly. Blunt is fine. Demeaning is not.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, slurs, personal or political attacks, sexual attention,
publishing someone's private information, or persistent disruption after being
asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits and reviews — anywhere this
project is the subject.

## Reporting

Open an issue, or email the maintainer directly if it should not be public.
Reports are read by the maintainer only.

A maintainer may edit, hide or delete a contribution, or block an account, and
will say why when it is not obvious.
